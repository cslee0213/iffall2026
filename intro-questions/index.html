  6. The Hardest Working Font in Manhattan
    I thought this website was well designed for its use (a digital book), as it's very easy to use and designed in a way that makes the information easy to digest and fun to understand with its digital interactions and intuitive navigation bar.

  7. Very Good Films
    The simplicity in this site's design I think pretty effectively communicates its goal: to be a digital film catalogue for film lovers. The UI is intuitive, and easy to use, and despite its simplicity, the information is presented in a way that is easy to understand.

  8. SoundGo
    This is one of the coolest websites I've found recently, and I thought the gesture-controls were very easy to use and understand. Anyone can play around with chords with a webcam, and make some music!
