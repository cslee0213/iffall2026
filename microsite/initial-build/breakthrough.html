The Breakthrough

“Video Games”, 2011

In 2011 she uploaded homemade music videos for “Video Games” and “Blue Jeans” to YouTube. She had made the videos herself from old film clips and webcam footage. “Video Games” went viral and became a top-ten hit in several European countries.

She signed with Stranger Records, and later that year with Interscope and Polydor. In October 2011 she won the Q Award for Next Big Thing.

Born to Die, 2012

Her first major-label album, Born to Die, was released on January 27, 2012. It debuted at No. 2 on the US Billboard 200 and No. 1 in the UK, and it went on to one of the longest chart runs by a woman in Billboard 200 history.

Paradise and beyond, 2012–2013

In November 2012 she released the Paradise EP, which included the single “Ride” and was nominated for the Grammy Award for Best Pop Vocal Album. In 2013 she released Tropico, a short film, and recorded “Young and Beautiful” for the film The Great Gatsby.

During these years she won two Brit Awards: International Breakthrough Act (2012) and International Female Solo Artist (2013). “Video Games” also won the Ivor Novello Award for Best Contemporary Song in 2012.
