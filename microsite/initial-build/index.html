Lana Del Rey

Who is
Lana Del Rey?

Elizabeth Woolridge Grant, born in Manhattan on June 21, 1985, records and performs as Lana Del Rey. Her songs are described to be slow, lush and cinematic. Her "aesthetic" is associated images of mid-century America: old Hollywood, open highways, the Pacific coast. She is widely counted among the most influential singer-songwriters of the twenty-first century.

She grew up in upstate New York and moved to Manhattan in 2005 to make music. Then, in 2011, a homemade video for “Video Games” spread across the internet and led to a deal with Interscope and Polydor. Her major-label debut, Born to Die (2012), went on to be one of the longest runs by a woman in the history of the Billboard 200.
