Sound & Style

Musical style

Her music is often described as cinematic. Critics have placed it in genres including baroque pop, dream pop, alternative pop and sadcore. Her early work combined orchestral arrangements with hip-hop beats. Later albums moved toward psychedelic rock, jazz, soft rock and folk. Her upcoming album, Stove, is influenced by country music.

Since 2019 she has worked closely with Jack Antonoff, starting with Norman Fucking Rockwell!

Voice

Her voice is usually described as a contralto. She is known for switching between a low, breathy register and a higher, softer one, sometimes within the same song.

Themes and image

Her songs often deal with tragic romance, glamour and melancholy. They make frequent references to pop culture and to American life in the 1950s and 1960s, especially old Hollywood. She often directs or edits her own music videos, and they use a vintage, film-like style.

Influences

She has named many influences, including Frank Sinatra, Elvis Presley, Billie Holiday, Nina Simone, Bob Dylan, Leonard Cohen, Joni Mitchell, Lou Reed and Kurt Cobain, as well as the poets Walt Whitman and Allen Ginsberg.

Reception and legacy

Some critics have argued that her songs romanticize unhealthy relationships, a claim she has disputed. She is widely seen as one of the most influential singer-songwriters of her generation. Artists such as Billie Eilish, Lorde and Olivia Rodrigo have named her as an influence.
